## Business context

I contributed to a cross-department B2B credit-management and customer-evaluation machine-learning proof of concept for Canon Optics China. The scenario connected company-profile information, financial data, and transaction data with a business question: how can an enterprise assess overdue risk and support credit-limit decisions with a more structured evidence base?

This page describes a professional POC. It does not publish customer data, proprietary model details, feature values, decision thresholds, accuracy figures, or confidential proposal material.

## My contribution

My contributions covered several parts of the applied-ML workflow:

- model construction using a scorecard-oriented approach;
- part of the data preprocessing across company, financial, and transaction information;
- technical validation of the resulting workflow;
- architecture and modeling discussions with the wider project group;
- participation in meetings and presentations whose results supported a formal customer proposal opportunity.

The wording “contributed” is intentional. The work was collaborative and cross-departmental; it is evidence of hands-on professional Applied ML participation, not a claim that I was the sole model owner.

## Data-to-decision workflow

The POC connected a business question to a reviewable technical path:

1. Identify the customer-evaluation and credit-management decision context.
2. Prepare and validate the available company, financial, and transaction data.
3. Construct and discuss a scorecard-oriented model approach.
4. Validate the technical workflow and its assumptions.
5. Communicate findings and limitations across project participants.
6. Use the work as supporting evidence for a formal proposal discussion.

The important engineering lesson is that model work depends on data contracts, preprocessing decisions, validation, and communication with the people who will interpret the decision. A model output by itself is not a complete credit-management system.

## What this demonstrates

This POC strengthens the professional evidence for:

- Applied ML and scorecard methodology;
- financial and business-data preparation;
- technical validation and architecture discussion;
- data-to-decision workflows in a business setting;
- cross-functional and customer-facing communication.

It connects naturally to the personal OpenScorecard case study, but the two are not the same project. OpenScorecard is a private-source portfolio lab using synthetic or sanitized evidence; this page records a professional POC with a separate disclosure boundary.

## Boundary

This work must remain labeled as a proof of concept. It is not a production ML platform, a deployed underwriting service, or mature production GenAI. No model accuracy, portfolio scale, credit-loss reduction, revenue impact, or deployment claim is made without separate evidence.
